Concrete Coating for Garage Floors in Tampa Bay

"Concrete coating" covers any protective floor system applied over a garage slab. For most Tampa Bay homeowners, Lifetime Garage Flooring installs a professional-grade polyaspartic coating — a fast-curing, UV-stable system that resists fading and holds up to daily garage use better than standard epoxy alone.

Benefits of Polyaspartic Concrete Coating

Fast Cure Time

Polyaspartic coatings cure in hours, not days, so your garage is back in use fast.

UV-Stable Finish

Won't yellow or fade in direct sunlight, unlike many epoxy-only systems.

Durable & Impact-Resistant

Withstands hot tires, dropped tools, and daily garage wear.

Lifetime Warranty

Every concrete coating installation is backed by our lifetime warranty.

Our Concrete Coating Process

1

Concrete Prep

We grind and repair the existing concrete to create a clean, sound surface for the coating to bond to.

2

Polyaspartic Base Coat

A professional-grade polyaspartic system is applied for a durable, fast-curing bond.

3

Decorative Flake (Optional)

Choose from custom color flake blends for a showroom-quality finish.

4

Fast-Cure Topcoat

A UV-stable topcoat seals the system — most garages are ready to use the same day.
