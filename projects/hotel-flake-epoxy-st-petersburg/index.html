Flake Epoxy Flooring with Integrated Cove Base

Location: St. Petersburg, FL  |  Scope: 1,500 sq ft  |  System: Flake Epoxy with Integrated Cove Base

This large, high-traffic kitchen space needed a flooring system that could stand up to constant foot traffic and frequent cleaning, while meeting strict hygiene requirements. We installed a heavy-duty flake epoxy system across 1,500 sq ft, with an integrated cove base carrying the coating up the wall a few inches at every floor-wall junction — eliminating hard corners where dirt and moisture could otherwise collect, for a seamless, easy-to-clean surface.
